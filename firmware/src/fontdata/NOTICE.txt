The production builtin face is m4_center_kernel_16x16.bin, generated from
the external pixel TTF 标准像素粗.ttf (SHA-256
9507b4d3e915455afadfa688e8ea515abf816bce06f76346ee356f0f38810574) with
firmware/scripts/generate_m4_center_kernel.py. CJK uses 16x16 center-kernel
occupancy; Latin/punct uses 16x16 occupancy sampled from the same TTF.
The TTF is not vendored. Runtime TTF/OTF from SD/FONT remain available for
reader content only. m4_native_grid_15x16.bin is host-test residue and is
not embedded in the device image.
