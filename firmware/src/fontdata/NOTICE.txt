The tracked m4_native_grid_15x16.bin derivative is generated from the
external pixel TTF 标准像素粗.ttf (SHA-256
9507b4d3e915455afadfa688e8ea515abf816bce06f76346ee356f0f38810574) with
firmware/scripts/generate_m4_native_grid.py. The source TTF is not vendored.
That blob is a ranked-bitset 15x16 1-bit corpus plus a 32-glyph outlier
sidecar. It is the builtin system UI face and the default/fallback reader
face. Runtime TTF/OTF from SD/FONT remain available for reader content only.
