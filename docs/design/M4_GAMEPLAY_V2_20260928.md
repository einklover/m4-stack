## Findings in current main f3c47b6

Sokoban: 12 original small levels; the first three are near-identical straight corridors. UI draws plain solid-wall/rectangle-crate tiles and eight text buttons. Existing parser, movement, undo, corner detection and best-score persistence work, but the content and visual hierarchy lack a deliberate game design.
Connect / Shisen-Sho: every pair starts horizontally adjacent; only symbol labels shuffle. The route algorithm is real, but the opening deal is trivially solvable without utilizing it.
Sliding: picture mode draws generic geometric diamond fragments rather than a coherent picture.
Nonogram: 13 geometric pictures, mostly crosses, frames and stripes; a host uniqueness tester exists.
Bulls and Cows: working rule engine and numeric entry, but little strategic feedback and a seven-row text keypad.
These shortcomings are acceptance/design problems, not evidence of intentional underperformance.

## A. Sokoban: complete this stage and prove it before moving on

Experience: clear entry screen with three chapters, 初识搬运 / 绕路布局 / 次序机关, eight original puzzles per chapter, 24 total. Level selection is paginated eight per page, shows cleared/best and is tappable. Gameplay uses a centered legible board (480x800, roughly y90..605), clear header with level/moves/pushes/best, walls with bold monochrome hatch or solid edge, distinct goal dot, crate X/handles, crate-on-goal marking, and a readable round directional player. One static frame per action; never require animation or color.

Controls: D-pad physical keys and touch arrows with >=52px hit targets; Undo, Restart, Levels and optionally Hint are discoverable and do not overlap the board. Confirm currently maps to Undo and hardware Back exits; preserve unless a better consistent mapping is tested. Tap a reachable floor tile to walk there via a bounded no-push board BFS; touching blocked tile does not change the state or consume undo. Optionally tap an adjacent crate for one valid push. Board must fit or the map is rejected at packaging, not clipped. Dead corner and level win are visually distinct; win has an obvious 下一关 button. Help includes a small example board rather than only text.

Levels: curate 24 **distinct original** 5x5..12x10 maps, at least 14 with 2+ crates and at least 5 with 3+ crates. The first 1-3 may teach basics, but later maps must teach route ordering, temporary displacement, corner avoidance, and interactions between crates. At least 6 late levels must have an optimal solution requiring >=6 pushes. Use a bounded **host-only** push-state or Sokoban BFS to prove every shipped level is solvable, record shortest pushes and steps, and reject duplicate maps including simple mirror/rotation. Do not run the solver on the e-ink device. If 24 maps cannot be proven, ship a smaller fully verified first chapter and disclose exact scope rather than add padding.

Progress: retain old saved scores where the exact old map fingerprint matches. Version the new format so changing level order cannot misattribute a prior best. Save a bounded current-level checkpoint on explicit exit and successful pushes, not every walking step, and test state restoration and undo. Avoid unnecessary full redraws and large Lua allocation.

QA: existing movement and corner tests plus complete bundled-level solvability proof, replay+undo, touch-navigation, chapter pagination, win-next hit target, v1 progress compatibility, corrupted save fallback. Capture deterministic UI trace/screenshots (level picker, mid-board with 2+ crates, deadlock and victory) at 480x800 via existing simulator or render host; if visual evidence cannot be produced, say so explicitly. Passing a source assertion alone is not signoff.

## B. Connect / 连连看: only after A

Remove the opening grid of adjacent horizontal pairs. Use reverse-removal pair placement, with only placements that already have a valid <=2-turn path through empty/outside-border cells, keeping a verified inverse removal order. Bounded retries or precomputed offline decks are fine, but no unbounded on-device search. Keep 4x6 beginner and 6x8 intermediate. Each shipped deck must replay all legal pair removals to empty in host tests; >=30% of opening pairs nonadjacent, with one/two-turn and border paths represented across the curated decks. Mixed distinct one-bit tile symbols rather than relying only on Latin letters. Clear selection feedback, visible removal path, guaranteed hint and safe shuffle, preserving board on failed shuffle. Save/load and undo tested. Capture nontrivial initial and mid-game board visuals.

## C. Subsequent small independent improvements

Sliding puzzle: at least three coherent original black/white illustrations (rocket, robot, mountain hut) actually sliced into image tiles with a thumbnail reference; no diamond placeholder. Assert image tiles reassemble exactly and scrambled board is solvable. Keep number mode.
Nonogram: 24 intentional pixel-art pictures (5x5/10x10/15x15 when readable); host uniqueness proof, meaningful difficulty order, separate Fill/X touch modes and assisted-clear accounting.
Bulls/Cows: readable guess history and elimination notes; a more compact keypad; preserve 3/4/5-digit rules and no leading zero. If candidate counts appear, calculate from guess history, never from the hidden secret.
